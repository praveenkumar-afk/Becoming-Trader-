Rule Book
📅Trading Days
▾
Tuesday & Thursday — mandatory
Other days — optional, genuine setup only
Busy days — 1 trade only, 9:15–10:30 AM
🎯Entry Rules
▾
Pre-plan before session starts
CPR above = buy only · CPR below = sell only
Entry only after 12:30 PM (except busy days)
Wait for swing formation — SL is swing
Wait for support / resistance confirmation
Focus on setup — not on profit
⚡Circuit Breakers
▾
2 profitable trades = session over immediately
3 trades = session over. No exceptions.
₹50K profit = close terminal immediately
₹50K loss = stop, no reload
₹1L P&L = next trading day off
💰Capital Rules
▾
50% of capital per trade
Max ₹50K reload/day — wife's account only
